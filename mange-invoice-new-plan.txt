## 3. PRODUCT DEFINITION

Build a personal Invoice & Expense Management Application.

The application allows individual customers to upload invoices, receipts, purchase photographs, hospital bills, contractor invoices, and PDF documents.

The application recognizes the uploaded documents, extracts their information, converts the information into a readable digital format, and organizes everything automatically.

Customers can subsequently:

- View original invoices.
- View extracted digital invoices.
- Search historical documents.
- Understand their spending.
- Generate expense reports.
- Export reports and invoices.
- Download historical documents.

### Account Model

This is NOT a multi-tenant business/workspace platform.

Do not implement organization management, company workspaces, organization switching, or business tenant administration.

Individual customer accounts must have isolated personal data.

Every customer's documents, invoice records, categories, reports, and subscription information must belong to that customer's account.

No customer must ever be able to access another customer's private information.

## 9. AI INVOICE RECOGNITION

- Discounts.
- Taxes.
- Subtotal.
- Grand total.
- Payment information.
- Other relevant information.

### Human-Readable Product Names

This is a critical requirement.

Retail receipts frequently contain abbreviated descriptions, internal product codes, or shortened product names.

Do not simply display the raw receipt description as the final product name.

The application must attempt to identify and present understandable product and service names.

Examples:

- BANANA → Bananas.
- APPLE SHP → Apple Shape, only when the product identity can be verified.
- Abbreviated retailer descriptions → readable product descriptions when identifiable.

Preserve both:

1. Original extracted description.
2. Human-readable description.

Never invent a product identity when it cannot be established.

If a product cannot be confidently identified, retain its original description and clearly indicate that the description is unverified.

The same requirements apply to unknown stores and vendors.

### Extraction Accuracy

The system must:

- Preserve original monetary values.
- Avoid inventing missing values.
- Identify uncertain fields.
- Distinguish missing information from zero.
- Identify duplicate uploads where possible.
- Validate invoice totals against available line items.
- Flag discrepancies for customer review.
